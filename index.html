Our Structured Construction Process

A battle-tested 6-stage lifecycle ensuring seamless transition from conceptual sketches to keys-in-hand occupancy.

01

Consultation

In-depth discussion of client requirements, site visit, soil bearing test review, budget alignment, and regulatory zoning checks.

Deliverable: Initial Feasibility & Project Charter
02

Planning & Design

Architectural 2D drawings, 3D structural BIM modeling, electrical/plumbing MEP schematics, and municipal sanction documentation.

Deliverable: Approved Blueprints & Permitting
03

Estimation & BOQ

Exhaustive itemized costing covering concrete volume, steel tonnage, labor wages, equipment hire, and legal milestone schedules.

Deliverable: Fixed-Price Contract & Gantt Chart
04

Construction

Excavation, foundation piling, RCC superstructure fabrication, brick masonry, MEP rough-ins, and architectural finishing works.

Deliverable: Weekly Photo & Milestone Reports
05

Quality Check

Non-destructive concrete ultrasonic testing, plumb alignment, plumbing hydrostatic pressure tests, and electrical load balancing.

Deliverable: Comprehensive QA/QC Audit Certificate
06

Handover & Warranty

Formal site walkthrough, handover of as-built architectural drawings, occupancy certificates, and initiation of warranty care.

Deliverable: Official Keys & 5-Year Warranty Binder
