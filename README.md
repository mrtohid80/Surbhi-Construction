# SURBHI CONSTRUCTIONS PVT. LTD. - Official Website

A high-performance, fully responsive, and professional multi-section web application built for **SURBHI CONSTRUCTIONS PVT. LTD.**, featuring an industrial civil engineering design aesthetic, smooth micro-animations, and full mobile optimization.

---

## 🏗️ Design System & Color Palette

- **Deep Navy Blue** (`#0B1E36`, `#142850`, `#071322`): Represents corporate governance, structural permanence, and trust.
- **Industrial Safety Gold & Orange** (`#F59E0B`, `#D97706`, `#EA580C`): Reflects heavy machinery, site vigilance, and dynamic energy.
- **Architectural Slate & Pure White** (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#64748B`): Provides crisp readability and modern cleanroom clarity.
- **Modern Typography**: Google Fonts **Outfit** (headings, stats, badges) + **Inter** (body, specifications).

---

## 🚀 Key Sections & Features Implemented

1. **HERO / HOME SECTION**:
   - Sticky Header with custom SVG architectural logo, full desktop navigation, mobile drawer menu, and quick CTA.
   - Headline: *"Building Quality. Creating Trust."*
   - Engaging introduction and dual CTA buttons ("Get a Quote" modal trigger & "Contact Us" scroll).
   - **Dynamic Statistics Counter**: Animated count-up with easing for *Projects Completed (250+)*, *Years of Experience (18+)*, *Clients Served (450+)*, and *Services Offered (12+)*.

2. **ABOUT US SECTION**:
   - Company introduction, two-decade heritage background, and key civil engineering highlights.
   - Interactive Vision & Mission cards.
   - Executive Management note with Board of Directors credentialing.
   - "Why clients choose us" checklist with checkmark accents.

3. **OUR SERVICES (Interactive Grid Layout)**:
   - 8 interactive cards with hover lift, accent borders, and custom icons:
     1. Residential Construction
     2. Commercial Construction
     3. Industrial Construction
     4. Renovation & Remodeling
     5. Civil Works
     6. Structural Work
     7. Interior & Finishing Work
     8. Project Management (PMC)
   - "Enquire Service" links automatically pre-fill the quote request modal with that specific service.

4. **PROJECT PHOTO GALLERY & LIGHTBOX**:
   - High-resolution interactive visual showcase featuring luxury villas, 3D elevations, commercial towers, and industrial PEB sites.
   - Dynamic category filter tabs: *All Works, Luxury Villas, 3D Elevations, Commercial, Interior & Finishes, Industrial & PEB, Ongoing Sites*.
   - Rich project cards with stakeholder credentialing (Client & Consultant Engineers tags: Er. Shaiban Patel, Er. Jafar Badeghar), specifications, and status badges.
   - Fullscreen Lightbox viewer with Next/Previous navigation, keyboard controls (Esc, Arrow keys), photo counters, and detailed captions.

5. **WHY CHOOSE US**:
   - Clean UI cards with icons for:
     - Quality Construction (ISO 9001:2015 & Grade-53 cement)
     - Experienced Team (Certified civil engineers & architects)
     - Timely Completion (Gantt/CPM scheduling & milestone audits)
     - Transparent Pricing (Itemized fixed-price BOQ)
     - Safety First (OSHA compliant, zero-accident culture)
     - Customer Satisfaction (5-year structural warranty)

6. **OUR PROCESS (Visual Timeline / Flowchart)**:
   - 6-step lifecycle:
     1. Consultation & Feasibility
     2. Planning & 3D BIM Design
     3. Estimation & Itemized BOQ
     4. Construction & Superstructure
     5. Multi-Stage Quality Check (QA/QC)
     6. Handover & 5-Year Warranty

7. **TESTIMONIALS (Carousel / Slider)**:
   - Smooth animated carousel with authentic client reviews.
   - Star ratings, client names, designations, and project tags.
   - Controls: Next/Prev arrows, pagination dots, autoplay (6s interval), and pause-on-hover.

8. **FAQ (Accordion Style)**:
   - Interactive accordion with expand/collapse animations and accessible ARIA attributes.
   - Covers types of construction, turnkey services, estimate process, locations served, and construction timelines.

9. **CONTACT US & FOOTER**:
   - Corporate headquarters address, direct telephone lines, and email.
   - Embedded Google Maps location frame.
   - **Functional Quote & Contact Request Form** with real-time field validation, error highlights, and submission feedback.
   - Comprehensive footer with quick links, service catalog, copyright notice, legal modals, and a **Brochure Download** generator.

⭐ **GLOBAL FEATURES**:
- **Floating WhatsApp button** (bottom right) with direct pre-filled WhatsApp link.
- **Mobile Sticky Quick-Action Bar** ("Call Now", "Get Quote", "WhatsApp").
- **Back to Top Button** with smooth scroll.
- **Stylized 404 Error Page Template** (`404.html`) with construction crane theme and recovery navigation.
- **Scroll-triggered animations** via `IntersectionObserver`.

## 💻 How to View & Preview

Simply open `index.html` directly in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari):

```bash
# Option 1: Open directly via your file manager or double-click index.html

# Option 2: Using Python built-in HTTP server (optional)
python -m http.server 8000
# Then visit http://localhost:8000
```
